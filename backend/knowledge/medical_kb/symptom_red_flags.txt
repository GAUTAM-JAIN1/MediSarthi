MEDISARTHI — SYMPTOM URGENCY REFERENCE
(Shared knowledge base for SymptomTriageCrew. General triage reference —
grounds the triage_nurse agent's own judgement, does not replace it. Note:
safety_guardrails.py already hardcodes an instant pre-check for the most
unambiguous emergencies — chest pain, can't breathe, stroke signs, heavy
bleeding, suicidal ideation, obstetric emergencies — bypassing this crew
entirely for those cases. This file is for everything the LLM triage agent
itself still needs to reason about: the wider, less clear-cut range of
presentations the hardcoded keyword list deliberately doesn't try to cover.)

== FEVER ==

Home Care territory: Fever <102°F in an otherwise well adult, responding to
paracetamol, alert and drinking fluids normally, no other worrying signs.

See a Doctor Soon: Fever persisting >3 days despite medicine, fever with
significant body ache/weakness limiting daily activity, fever in someone
with a chronic condition (diabetes, heart disease) that could complicate
recovery.

Emergency territory: Fever with stiff neck and severe headache, fever with
confusion or difficulty staying awake, very high fever (>104°F) not
responding to medicine, fever in an infant under 3 months of age.

== ABDOMINAL PAIN ==

Home Care: Mild, generalized discomfort after a heavy/spicy meal, improving
over hours, no fever, no vomiting.

See a Doctor Soon: Persistent pain lasting >24-48 hours, pain with
low-grade fever, pain that's localized and worsening rather than improving.

Emergency: Sudden severe pain, pain with rigid/board-like abdomen, pain
localized to lower right side with fever (possible appendicitis), pain with
persistent vomiting and inability to keep fluids down, pain during pregnancy
with bleeding.

== RESPIRATORY SYMPTOMS ==

Home Care: Mild cough/congestion, no fever or low-grade only, breathing
comfortably at rest and with mild activity.

See a Doctor Soon: Cough persisting beyond 2 weeks, cough with colored
(green/yellow) phlegm and fever, mild breathlessness only with significant
exertion that's new for the person.

Emergency: Breathlessness at rest, blue lips/fingertips, unable to speak in
full sentences due to breathlessness, chest tightness with breathlessness
(this overlaps with the hardcoded guardrail — noted for completeness).

== HEADACHE ==

Home Care: Typical tension-type headache, responds to usual pain relief,
similar to headaches the person has had before.

See a Doctor Soon: New headache pattern different from usual, headache
with mild visual disturbance, frequent headaches needing daily medicine.

Emergency: Sudden "worst headache of my life," headache with fever and
neck stiffness, headache with confusion/slurred speech/weakness on one
side (overlaps with stroke guardrail keywords).

== SKIN / RASH ==

Home Care: Mild localized rash, itching without spreading, no fever, known
trigger (e.g., new soap, mild allergy).

See a Doctor Soon: Rash spreading over days, rash with mild swelling, rash
that isn't improving with usual antihistamines/creams after several days.

Emergency: Rash with facial/throat swelling, rash with difficulty breathing,
rapidly spreading rash with high fever (possible serious infection or severe
allergic reaction).

== GENERAL / VAGUE SYMPTOMS ==

Home Care: Mild fatigue, feeling "off," minor aches — especially with an
identifiable cause (poor sleep, stress, recent minor illness).

See a Doctor Soon: Unexplained fatigue lasting >2 weeks, unexplained weight
loss, symptoms that are mild individually but persistent and unexplained.

Emergency: Sudden severe weakness on one side of the body, sudden confusion
in someone previously alert, fainting/loss of consciousness even if brief
and now "back to normal" (needs same-day evaluation, not necessarily
ambulance-level unless recurrent or with injury).

== GENERAL NOTES FOR THE AGENT ==

- These buckets describe common patterns, not rules — the agent should
  still weigh the patient's full description (duration, associated symptoms,
  age, known conditions) rather than pattern-matching a single symptom word.
- When a description sits between two buckets, the agent should lean toward
  the higher urgency level, consistent with the "when in doubt, escalate"
  instruction already in its task config.
- This file intentionally does not repeat the hardcoded guardrail's exact
  keyword list — that layer already handles the clearest cases before this
  crew is even invoked. This file exists for the greyer, more common
  presentations where judgement (not a keyword match) is actually needed.
